一 个 工 房 · 一 本 日 志 · 与 中 间 慢 慢 长 出 来 的 小 软 件

My name is here, and this is where I keep records of the small pieces of software I ship — written mostly at night, mostly for myself, occasionally for friends who asked nicely.

这里是我记录每一次软件发布的地方。多数在夜里写成，多数写给自己用，偶尔写给那些客气地问过我的朋友。

关 于 我— About the Maker

I make small, single-purpose tools — text editors, terminals, clocks, clippers — and I write them slowly, the way one might whittle a spoon. None of them are trying to win. They are trying to fit the hand.

我做小工具：编辑器、终端、时钟、剪贴簿。慢慢地做，像削一柄木勺。它们不打算赢，它们打算 合手。

This site is the journal that goes with the work. Each entry is a release: what changed, what broke, what I learned this round.

这个站点是工作日志。每一条都对应一次发布：改了什么，坏了什么，这一轮学到了什么。
